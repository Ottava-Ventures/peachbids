PeachBids will be a weekday email list of public Georgia cleaning, janitorial and grounds bids for contractors. It is launching soon. This page lists the sources the list will draw from, by region, and what we don’t read.

For each county and city, we will read the agency’s own bid page. Some agencies take responses on a vendor platform, which we don’t read.

What we don’t read

Some agencies post bids on vendor platforms PeachBids doesn’t read. Georgia law requires counties, cities and school boards to post bids for goods or services of $100,000 or more on the Georgia Procurement Registry, which we will read every weekday. Other bids posted only on those platforms won’t appear, so check the agencies you bid with directly.

Always confirm scope, licensing and eligibility requirements on the original posting.
