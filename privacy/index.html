Privacy

Updated: October 18, 2026. When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address and whether you checked the founding-member box. The signup form runs on Google Forms, which stores your answers for us. We use your email for launch updates, a free weekly teaser, and once we launch, the weekday bid list. If you checked the founding-member box, we may also email you about founding-member pricing when paid alerts launch. We don't sell your email or share it with anyone for their marketing. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security; we don't use analytics or tracking cookies. To unsubscribe, use the link in any email or reply "unsubscribe" and we'll remove you.

How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you unsubscribe, we keep only your email address on a do-not-email list so we never email you again. Questions or deletion requests: hello@peachbids.com.
