Support

Questions, billing and corrections

How to reach us

Email hello@peachbids.com

It helps to tell us which Georgia counties you bid in and your trade, such as janitorial, custodial, floor care, landscaping or grounds maintenance.

Billing

Paid subscriptions aren’t open yet. Once they are, Stripe will process payments, and your payment receipts will come from Stripe by email. To cancel a subscription, use this cancellation email link from the address on your account: Cancel my PeachBids subscription

Refund terms are in our Terms of Service.

How Stripe handles your payment details is on our Privacy & disclosures page.

Unsubscribing

Every email from PeachBids includes an unsubscribe link. Click it and we’ll take you off the list.

Unsubscribing stops the emails. If you have a paid subscription, cancel it under Billing so it doesn’t renew.

Report a wrong listing

If a bid we list has a wrong date, a broken link or isn’t cleaning or grounds work, email hello@peachbids.com with the bid title and the agency. We’ll check the original posting and correct the listing if it’s wrong.

What we can’t help with

We can’t write bids for you or give legal advice. Before you bid, confirm the scope, licensing and eligibility rules in the agency’s original posting.
