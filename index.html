How it will work

Many cities, counties and school districts post bids on their own portals. PeachBids will add them to one list as we add sources.

  1. 1

    We’ll check public Georgia bid postings each weekday.

    We’ll start with the Georgia Procurement Registry and Gwinnett County’s bid page, and we’ll name each new source here when it’s live.

  2. 2

    We’ll filter for cleaning and grounds work and remove duplicates.

    Cleaning and grounds bids only: janitorial, custodial, floor care, facility cleaning, landscaping and grounds maintenance. No noise.

  3. 3

    You’ll get one short list with links to the original postings.

    Each listing will show the agency, scope, due date and a link to the original posting.

    Once we launch: one short email on mornings with new bids. Unsubscribe any time.

Questions

Are you affiliated with the State of Georgia?

No. Details are on our Privacy & disclosures page.

How is this different from the state registry?

The Georgia Procurement Registry will be our main source. At launch, PeachBids will read it and Gwinnett County’s bid page, pick out janitorial and grounds work, and send you a short list with a link to each original posting. We plan to add county, city and school sources and will name each one here when it’s live.

Which areas will you cover?

Georgia only to start.

What will it cost?

Not set yet. We will announce it before launch.

Will you help write bids?

No. Each listing will link to the original posting. We don’t write bids or give legal advice.

Who’s behind PeachBids

I’m Michael, and I run a small business in metro Atlanta. For a contractor running a lean operation, every hour spent hunting down leads is an hour not spent pricing jobs, managing crews, or getting paid.

Georgia’s public cleaning and grounds bids are buried across dozens of separate state, county, city, and school board websites. I’m building PeachBids so a local contractor doesn’t need a full‑time desk job just to see what’s out for bid.
